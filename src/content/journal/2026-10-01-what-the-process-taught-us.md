Looking back across the conversations, experiments, false starts and the alpha installation, a few lessons kept returning.

## Never tell people what to feel

Being told to feel connected is the fastest way not to. Small rituals and room for people's own stories work better.

[Read *Rituals, not instructions* →](/cloud-belonging-website/process/2026-05-19-rituals-not-instructions/)

## Belonging is not control

Interactive work usually rewards control. We wanted people to play a role alongside the cloud, breathing with it rather than steering it.

[Read *Belonging is not control* →](/cloud-belonging-website/process/2026-06-10-belonging-not-control/)

## Make it travel home

Asking the experience to carry into everyday life kept it minimal. The next cloud you see should bring a little of it back.

[Read *Something you take home* →](/cloud-belonging-website/process/2026-05-13-something-you-take-home/)

## How people are welcomed shapes what they feel

*Réespiration* showed a wide range of responses, from deep relaxation to boredom. Priming, guidance and a robust signal matter as much as the artwork.

[Read *Quand le ciel recule* →](/cloud-belonging-website/process/2026-07-02-quand-le-ciel-recule/)

## A breath sensor has to really hear a breath

Narrow stretch bands barely registered above the noise. A 20 cm band gave a clear, readable wave.

[Read *A belt that listens* →](/cloud-belonging-website/process/2026-06-12-a-belt-that-listens/)

## The material has to hide the technology

Mesh was too opaque and tulle would not hold a shape. Layers of soft fibre over a mesh shell finally turned hundreds of LEDs into one glow.

[Read *The alpha cloud* →](/cloud-belonging-website/process/2026-07-30-the-alpha-cloud/)

## A bond before the breath

Six planned movements became three. The magical twilight gives the cloud a character, so breathing with it means more.

[Read *The final score: the sounds of the installation* →](/cloud-belonging-website/process/2026-09-24-the-final-score/)

## Design an adjacent possible

Offer something people can discover, lean into and make their own, with many ways in.
