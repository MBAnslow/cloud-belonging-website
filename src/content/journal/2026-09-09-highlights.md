Separate parameters mix direct light from the sun with ambient light. Ducking adjusts how the shadows look in that mix, and a rim parameter sets what the edge of the cloud should look like. Bump mapping plays a role here too, so those lights follow the cloud’s shape rather than falling evenly across each flat plane.
