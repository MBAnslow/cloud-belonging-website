After the [mesh](/cloud-belonging-website/process/2026-05-21-mesh-and-tulle/), we tried something much softer.

Tulle was the surprise. It is **very light**, looks surprisingly good, and is almost transparent in a single layer. Bunched up, it starts to look like a small cloud. Run down a corridor, it trails behind like weather.

It became a candidate for the cloud's filling, and later its skin.

Tulle also took projected light well. We came back to that a month later, when a [projector on the floor turned us into the sun](/cloud-belonging-website/process/2026-06-24-breath-as-sound/).
