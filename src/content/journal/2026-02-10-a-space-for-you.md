The project began as a proposal Zéphir wrote for the Art & Cognition course at ENS-PSL, after a session at Sony CSL Paris. Its question: **to what extent can art and technology support the discovery of mindful practices, and put a little peace back into an increasingly chaotic world?** A second aim was to measure its effect on attention, emotional regulation and relaxation.

## Where it came from

Zéphir had spent the previous year running a pilot study on *Réespiration*, an installation developed between EnsadLab and the AP-HP Pitié-Salpêtrière hospital. A chest-like object breathes with the visitor, whose breath is read by a thermal camera, while light and music follow the same rhythm.

The first accounts were striking in their **heterogeneity**. Some people described deep relaxation, even meditative or hypnotic-like states. Others found the same experience oppressive or disengaging. An immersive work doesn't produce a single effect. It meets whoever arrives.

## What the new proposal changed

Compared with *Réespiration*, *A Space For You* would be:

- **public**: somewhere anyone could walk into, not a hidden hospital room;
- **light**: modular enough to set up anywhere, even outdoors with headphones;
- **collective**: responsive to a group, not only to one person's breath;
- **primed**: testing whether a welcome and a guided voice deepen the experience.

## A room in three sequences

The room is simple: beanbags spaced out, smart lights on the ceiling, surround sound, and sensors for heart rate, breath and skin response.

1. **Explore.** A performer welcomes people and invites them to wander. Light and music follow the group's physiology, softening towards low violet as the room relaxes. Anyone can whisper how they feel into a small microphone, and the light changes with it.
2. **Contemplate.** Everyone lies down. The light and sound stop following and start leading, slowing towards calm, with a 15-minute guided meditation written with a teacher from the Plum Village tradition.
3. **Land.** The light brightens to wake people gently, and they can stay to talk.

The light would be composed with [Funiki](https://csl.sony.fr/projects/funiki/), Sony CSL's system for dynamic atmospheres, which is how the project first reached us. The music would use the breath-synced score from *Réespiration*, driven by the group's average breathing.

## And a study

The proposal also sketched an experiment: the full experience against the same room without the guided meditation, measuring anxiety, state mindfulness, heart and breath before and after.

> A space to pause, for individual introspection, grounding and relaxation, but collectively, so participants can exchange on their experiences afterwards.

That word *collectively* is the seed that later grew into belonging, and the soft ceiling of light eventually became a cloud.
