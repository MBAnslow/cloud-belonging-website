A white hexagonal mesh went over the pyramid to give the fibre something to hold on to, and to keep it off the LEDs.   
  
We learned the craft of creating the outer shell by trial and error. Incrementally combining together pieces of mesh and altering its structure in various ways:

1. Pinch the manifold and hold it in place with zipties. 
2. Push sticks through the mesh along planes to hold that structure. 
3. Cut into the manifold to curve the surface or create more abrupt deformations and ziptie along along the cut edge. 

It was important to avoid having too many overlapping pieces of the mesh when curving it as we needed to pull the cotton through the mesh. However, prematurely trimming the overlapping mesh could be problematic if we wanted to adjust the hull of the cloud later.
